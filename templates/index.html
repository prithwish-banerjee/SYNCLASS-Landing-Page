Smart attendance platform

Take attendance from one class photo.

SYN Class recognizes students by face or voice and keeps every record in one place. Students join a subject with a QR code, so there is no roster to type in.

A better way to take attendance

Three ways to mark who is in the room

Teachers pick the method that suits the class. Students register their face and voice once and reuse them for every session.

Face analysis

Upload a class photo and SYN Class matches each face against enrolled students. You review the report before anything is saved.

Sequential voice ID

Students say "Present" one by one. Each voice is compared with the embedding stored at enrollment.

QR-driven roster

Every subject has a code, a join link and a QR code. Students scan it and they are enrolled, with no manual entry for the teacher.
